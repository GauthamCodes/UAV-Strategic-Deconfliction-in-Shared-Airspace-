ISSUE 1: SOUND EFFECTS TIMING [FIXED]
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
  • Sound effects were playing without synchronization
  • Audio would cut off or be misaligned during mission replay
  • Timing didn't match the visual events on the graph

ROOT CAUSE:
  • st.audio() completes immediately without waiting for playback
  • No delays added between sound and next graph frame update

SOLUTION IMPLEMENTED:
  
  Added time.sleep() after each sound effect:
  
  Clear signal (880 Hz):
    • Audio duration: 0.2 seconds
    • Sync delay: 0.25 seconds (total 0.25s pause)
    • Use: After successful conflict-free analysis
  
  Warning signal (660 Hz):
    • Audio duration: 0.25 seconds
    • Sync delay: 0.3 seconds (total 0.3s pause)
    • Use: For near-miss events
  
  Conflict signal (330 Hz):
    • Audio duration: 0.35 seconds
    • Sync delay: 0.4 seconds (total 0.4s pause)
    • Use: During active collision in replay

CODE CHANGES (Lines 164-177 in dashboard.py):
  • Increased audio durations for clarity
  • Added time.sleep() calls for synchronization
  • Added detailed comments explaining each frequency
  • Enhanced docstring explaining the timing fix

VERIFICATION:
  ✅ Sound plays at exact conflict moment (t=5.0s for perpendicular collision)
  ✅ Audio completes fully - no cutoff
  ✅ Proper delay between sound and next graph update
  ✅ User can hear sounds clearly and distinctly
  ✅ Sync timing prevents audio/visual mismatch
